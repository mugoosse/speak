# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who want a private, local macOS dictation tool that stays out of the way until they invoke its shortcut. The menu bar is the primary persistent surface.

## Product Purpose

Speak turns dictated speech into text locally. Its status glyph must communicate ready, recording, and unavailable states in a crowded macOS menu bar.

## Brand Personality

Warm, expressive, crafted. Speak is the red half of the Good Pair shared with Listen: a friendly monkey speaking through an open mouth.

## Anti-references

Generic microphone or chat-bubble symbols; overly detailed miniature mascots; decorative status-bar artwork that loses clarity at 16 points.

## Design Principles

- Keep the full character in the Dock and product storytelling, not the status bar.
- Use the Good Pair's shared square seal in the menu bar: 言 (speak) for Speak.
- Preserve macOS template-icon restraint while retaining a recognisable family cue.
- Let recording state add information without changing the icon vocabulary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use monochrome template imagery that adapts to macOS light and dark menu bars. Do not communicate status by colour alone; maintain a distinct recording silhouette and descriptive tooltips.
